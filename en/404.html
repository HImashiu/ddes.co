  1. AI have a lot and an ideaStart with the structural design. If the project is large, add a review by an independent engineer.
  2. BI am about to file for the permitIf the lot allows more than 2,000 m² to be built, the curaduría urbana (the building-permit authority) asks for a signed statement from a reviewer independent of the designer.
  3. CI am about to start buildingHire technical supervision before the first pour. Without it there is no Certificate of Occupancy.
  4. DWhat does the law require?Give us the area, the use and the number of floors, and we tell you which review, which supervision and which documents you need.
  5. EMy building has cracksA first visit tells you whether there is an immediate risk. Testing tells you the cause and what the repair will cost.

DDES is a structural engineering firm based in Barranquilla, founded in 2020. It provides structural design, independent design review, technical supervision and construction oversight (interventoría) under the NSR-10, Colombia's seismic-resistant building code, and Law 1796 of 2016, in the Caribbean region and the rest of Colombia.

Services

What we do

We design, we review other firms' designs and we supervise construction.

Structural design

Concrete, steel and masonry buildings designed to the NSR-10, from the first concept to the reinforcement details.

Construction oversight

We represent the owner: quality, schedule and budget on public and private projects.

Technical supervision

Independent technical supervision under Law 1796 and Title I of the NSR-10, before every concrete pour.

Independent review

Review of structural designs by others, required on projects of more than 2,000 m².

Assessment and retrofit

Damage assessment, seismic vulnerability and retrofit design for existing buildings.

BIM and coordination

Structural models in Revit coordinated with architecture and MEP, with quantities taken from the model.
